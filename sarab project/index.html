Our Journey

A History of Restaurant

From humble beginnings to the city's most beloved restaurant — every chapter written with passion.

012012

Evolution of Restaurants

Sarab opens its first 20-seat diner on Flavor Street. Within three months, lines stretch around the block every evening as word of our food spreads.

022015

Fine Dining & The Concept

We introduced our signature tasting menu and hired our first Michelin-trained chef, elevating our craft to remarkable new heights.

032019

Modern Fast Food Origins

We launched our signature fast-food line, merging gourmet quality with speed and convenience. Within six months, we won three prestigious culinary awards.

042026

National Expansion

Now operating in eight cities across the US, with an online delivery platform handling more than 10,000 orders each week.
